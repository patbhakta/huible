row's subtitle span ±0.5 s; mismatched single-candidate clips dropped too.

still needs prototype calibration. Text macro-F1 ~flat, top-1 down on the
corrected (harder) text stratum — surprise .625 / sadness .40 carry it;

both-correct 10.8%, text-right-audio-wrong 6.7% — complementarity persists,
smaller than v2 claimed.
